## Observations (hand-written for this baseline)

- **Chunk recall is near its ceiling** on this corpus (R@10 0.97–0.99 on every path), so
  recall alone will barely move. Most generated questions name their law, product or
  meeting, which makes them easy. Use MRR/nDCG, cross-lingual recall, false-accept rate
  and response size to compare changes; a harder question subset is a follow-up.
- **Cross-lingual zh → English documents is the weakest type** (MCP R@10 0.80). q059 and
  q060 miss entirely (Chinese documents win). q061 and q064 show a candidate-depth
  problem: the gold leaf is at dense rank 26–29 and fused rank 50–53. The reranker puts it
  first when the trace and `top_k=20` fetch 60 candidates, but the default MCP call
  (`top_k=10` → 3×10 = 30 candidates, `RERANK_CANDIDATE_FACTOR`) never sends it to the
  reranker.
- **Unanswerable questions cannot be rejected by score.** Rerank scores are saturated:
  every answerable question's top score is ≥ 0.96, and 18 of 20 unanswerable questions
  score ≥ 0.97. That makes the false-accept rate 1.0 at both 0.25 and 0.5.
  `rerank.score_threshold` 0.25 filters almost nothing.
- **MCP responses are large and repetitive**: about 8.2k estimated tokens per call (p95
  13.4k). Every result is a merged parent or a ±5-neighbour expanded window (no plain
  leaves), and 48% of the text repeats within a response (56% for English questions).
- **Ranking:** the reranked leaf list (trace) has MRR@10 0.92 (paraphrase 0.81). The
  dense list holds the best gold hit in its top 10 for at least 92% of questions; BM25
  does so for at least 80%. BM25-only rescues are rare (0.5%), dense-only ones 7.6%.
- **Variance:** the two runs gave identical rankings for every question. Only latency and
  token counts moved (p50 latency ±32 ms).
