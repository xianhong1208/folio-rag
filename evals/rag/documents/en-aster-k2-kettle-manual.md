## 1. Safety Instructions

Read all instructions before first use and keep this manual for future reference.

- Only use the kettle with the power base supplied (model AB-20). Do not use the base with any other appliance.
- Never immerse the kettle, the power base or the cord in water or any other liquid.
- Do not fill the kettle above the MAX mark (1.7 L) or below the MIN mark (0.5 L). Overfilling may cause boiling water to be ejected from the spout.
- Do not open the lid while the water is boiling. Steam escaping from the lid can cause scalds.
- Children aged 8 years and older may use the kettle only under supervision. Keep the kettle and its cord out of reach of children under 8.
- If the supply cord is damaged, it must be replaced by the manufacturer or an authorised service centre to avoid a hazard.

## 2. Product Overview

The Aster K2 is a 1.7-litre stainless-steel kettle with precise temperature control from 40 °C to 100 °C in 1-degree steps. It connects to the Harborlight Home app over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (5 GHz networks are not supported) and can also be operated from the touch panel on the power base.

Main parts:

- Lid with one-touch release button
- Removable stainless-steel mesh limescale filter
- Water level window with MIN (0.5 L) and MAX (1.7 L) marks
- Power base with touch panel, 4-digit display and status ring
- Cord storage under the base (75 cm cord)

## 4. Getting Started

1. Remove all packaging and stickers.
2. Fill the kettle to the MAX mark, boil the water and pour it away. Repeat this twice to remove any manufacturing residue.
3. Place the power base on a dry, flat, heat-resistant surface at least 10 cm from the wall.
4. Plug in the power base. The display shows "--" and the status ring glows white.

### Connecting to the Harborlight Home app

1. Install the Harborlight Home app (iOS 16 or later, Android 11 or later).
2. Press and hold the Wi-Fi button on the base for 5 seconds until the status ring flashes blue.
3. In the app, tap "Add device", choose "Aster K2" and enter the password of your 2.4 GHz network.
4. Pairing normally takes less than 60 seconds. When it is complete the status ring turns solid blue for 3 seconds.

## 5. Using the Kettle

- Boil: press the power button once. The kettle heats to 100 °C and switches off automatically.
- Set a temperature: press the + or − buttons to choose a temperature between 40 °C and 100 °C, then press the power button.
- Presets: press the PRESET button to cycle through 70 °C (green tea), 80 °C (white tea), 85 °C (oolong), 90 °C (pour-over coffee) and 100 °C (black tea).
- Keep warm: press and hold the power button for 2 seconds. The kettle keeps the water at the selected temperature for up to 60 minutes. The keep-warm icon flashes while it is active.
- Schedule: in the app you can schedule a boil up to 24 hours in advance. The kettle must be on the base and filled above the MIN mark at the scheduled time; otherwise the schedule is cancelled and you receive a notification.
- Boil-dry protection: if the kettle is switched on without water, it switches off automatically and shows error E2.

## 6. Cleaning and Descaling

- Always unplug the power base and let the kettle cool down before cleaning.
- Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. Do not use abrasive cleaners or steel wool.
- Rinse the mesh filter under running water once a week.
- Descale the kettle every 4 weeks in hard-water areas, or every 3 months in soft-water areas. The app reminds you when descaling is due, based on the number of boils.

To descale: fill the kettle with 0.5 L of white vinegar and 1 L of water, start the DESCALE program (hold PRESET and − together for 3 seconds), let the solution stand for 30 minutes after the program ends, then pour it away and boil clean water twice. Citric acid (2 tablespoons per litre of water) can be used instead of vinegar.

## 7. Error Codes and Troubleshooting

| Code | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Temperature sensor fault | Unplug the base for 1 minute. If E1 returns, contact service. |
| E2 | Boil-dry protection triggered | Let the kettle cool for 10 minutes and add water above the MIN mark. |
| E3 | Kettle lifted during heating | Put the kettle back on the base and press the power button again. |
| E4 | Wi-Fi connection lost | Check the router, then press the Wi-Fi button for 5 seconds to reconnect. |
| E5 | Firmware update failed | Keep the kettle on the base; the update retries automatically within 24 hours. |

If the water tastes of plastic or metal after first use, boil and discard water three more times. If the lid does not open, check that nothing is caught under the hinge; do not force it.

## 8. Warranty and Service

Harborlight provides a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase for the kettle and the power base. The limescale filter and the lid seal are consumables and are covered for 6 months. Damage caused by limescale build-up, by use with a power base other than model AB-20, or by commercial use is not covered.

To make a warranty claim, register the product in the Harborlight Home app or at the service portal and keep your proof of purchase. Customer service: support@harborlight.example, +886-2-5550-0123 (Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00 Taiwan time).

## 9. Disposal

Do not dispose of this appliance with household waste. Take it to a collection point for the recycling of electrical and electronic equipment. The battery-free design means no battery needs to be removed before recycling.
