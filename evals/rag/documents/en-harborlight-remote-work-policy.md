# Harborlight Technologies — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Source: written for the Folio retrieval eval set (fictional company policy). License: MIT, same as this repository.

Policy owner: People Operations
Policy number: HR-POL-014
Effective date: 1 April 2026 (replaces HR-POL-009, "Work From Home Guidelines", 2023)
Applies to: all full-time and part-time employees of Harborlight Technologies Co., Ltd. in Taiwan. Contractors follow the terms of their service agreements.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when employees may work away from the office, how hybrid schedules are agreed, and which security, equipment and working-time rules apply. It supplements, and never overrides, the Labor Standards Act and the company's work rules.

## 2. Eligibility

- Employees who have completed their 3-month probation period may request a hybrid schedule.
- Roles that require physical presence (warehouse, repair centre, reception and lab technicians) are not eligible for regular remote work, but may work remotely on an ad-hoc basis with their manager's approval.
- An employee on a performance improvement plan may have remote work suspended for the duration of the plan.

## 3. Hybrid Schedule

- The standard hybrid schedule is 3 office days and 2 remote days per week.
- Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days: every team member is in the office on these days unless on leave.
- Employees may request a fully remote arrangement for up to 3 months in a calendar year for personal reasons (for example caring for a family member). Requests are made in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance and need approval from both the manager and the People Operations business partner.
- Working remotely from outside Taiwan is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and must be approved in advance by People Operations and Legal, because of tax and data-transfer rules.

## 4. Working Hours, Overtime and Availability

- Core hours are 10:00–16:00 Taiwan time. Outside core hours employees may arrange their own start and end times, within a normal working day of 8 hours.
- Remote work does not change the rules on working time. Overtime must be requested in the HR portal and approved by the manager before it is worked, whether in the office or at home. Overtime pay or compensatory time off follows the Labor Standards Act.
- Employees are not expected to read or answer work messages between 20:00 and 08:00, or on rest days, unless they are on the published on-call rota.
- On-call engineers receive an on-call allowance of NT$1,500 per week of on-call duty, in addition to pay for any time actually worked.

## 5. Equipment and Allowances

- The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset for home use. Equipment remains company property and must be returned within 5 working days after the employment ends.
- Employees on a hybrid schedule receive a one-time home office stipend of NT$6,000 for a chair, desk or lighting. The stipend is paid with the first salary after the hybrid schedule is approved, and is not paid again within 3 years.
- A monthly internet allowance of NT$800 is paid to employees who work remotely at least 4 days per month.
- Printing confidential documents at home is not allowed.

## 6. Information Security

- Connect to company systems only through the company VPN with multi-factor authentication enabled.
- Do not work on company data on public Wi-Fi without the VPN, and never on shared or family computers.
- Lock the screen whenever you step away from the device; the device locks automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 1 hour of discovery, by phone at extension 7110 or through the security channel.
- Customer personal data may be processed remotely only in the approved systems. Downloading customer lists to a personal device is a disciplinary offence.

## 7. Health and Safety

Employees working from home should set up a safe workstation. People Operations offers a 30-minute virtual ergonomics check on request. A work-related injury that happens at home during working hours must be reported to the manager and People Operations within 24 hours, in the same way as an injury in the office.

## 8. Review

People Operations reviews this policy every 12 months. Questions can be sent to people-ops@harborlight.example.
